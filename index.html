About The Experience

More than a run.

Color Run is designed to bring students, families, fitness enthusiasts, and the entire community together for an unforgettable day of vibrance and unity.

Whether you are sprinting for a personal best or walking alongside friends and loved ones, every step on our 5KM course represents health, joy, and communal renewal. No prior running experience required—everyone is welcome!
