COLOR RUN: THE ULTIMATE FESTIVAL

Experience the brightest 5-kilometer un-timed fun run in the world! Color runners are doused from head to toe in different colored powder at each kilometer. The fun continues at the finish line with a gigantic Color Festival featuring music, dancing, and massive color throws.
